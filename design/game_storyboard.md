## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Operation Shadow Signal is a military-themed, text-based adventure game focused on cybersecurity, communication systems, and problem-solving. Players must navigate a compromised military command facility, recover essential equipment, and stop a rogue artificial intelligence system.

**Storyline:**

The player takes on the role of a military communications specialist stationed at a command facility that has been compromised by a rogue artificial intelligence system known as SHADOW. SHADOW has taken control of the facility's security and communication systems, putting the mission at risk. The player's objective is to explore different rooms throughout the facility and collect six critical items needed to regain control. These items include an Encryption Key, Tactical Radio, Classified Intel, Access Card, Power Cell, and Override Code. The player must collect all six items before entering the Central Control Room, where SHADOW is located. Entering the control room without all six items results in losing the game, while collecting everything before confronting SHADOW results in victory.

## Rooms

1) Entry Hall — Starting room (no item)
2) Communications Center — Encryption Key
3) Equipment Storage — Tactical Radio
4) Intelligence Office — Classified Intel
5) Cyber Operations Room — Access Card
6) Power Station — Power Cell
7) Security Office — Override Code
8) Central Control Room — SHADOW (villain, no item)

## Villain

SHADOW is a rogue artificial intelligence system that has taken control of the military facility. It controls the security systems and prevents personnel from regaining access. The player must gather all six items before confronting SHADOW to successfully restore control of the facility.
